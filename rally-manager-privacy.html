Rally Manager

Privacy Policy
Effective date: May 30, 2026

1. Overview

Rally Manager ("the App") is an event registration application developed for iOS and Android. This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you. By using Rally Manager, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum information necessary to provide the App's features.

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and a hashed password. We do not collect your name, phone number, or payment information through the App.

Usage data. We collect anonymous, aggregate data about how features are used — such as session frequency and duration selections — to improve the App. This data is not linked to your identity.

Device data. We may collect basic device identifiers (operating system version, device type) for crash reporting and compatibility purposes.

Reminder preferences. Notification times you set are stored locally on your device and, if you are signed in, synced to our servers to restore your preferences across devices.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

Rally Manager does not collect:

  • Health or biometric data
  • Location data
  • Contacts or calendar data
  • Microphone or camera data
  • Any data from children under 13

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Document attendance at events you attend
  • Diagnose crashes and fix bugs
  • Understand aggregate usage patterns to improve the App

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your data to any third party.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: We use third-party services for hosting, analytics, and crash reporting. These providers are contractually prohibited from using your data for any purpose other than providing services to us.
  • Legal compliance: We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal process.
  • Business transfers: If Rally Manager is acquired or merged, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your data becomes subject to a materially different privacy policy.

6. Data Retention

We retain the information of your attendance in spreadsheets available only to the Board members of the event’s organization.

7. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (TLS) and hashed password storage. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to protecting your data.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent to data processing
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

9. Children's Privacy

Rally Manager is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy in the App and, where appropriate, by email. Your continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
